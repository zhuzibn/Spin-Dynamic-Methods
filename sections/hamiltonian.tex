\subsection{Atomistic Spin Hamiltonian}

\paragraph{Equation.}
A common classical spin Hamiltonian containing exchange, uniaxial anisotropy,
Dzyaloshinskii--Moriya interaction, and Zeeman energy is
\begin{align}
    \mathcal{H}
    ={}& -\frac{1}{2}\sum_{i\ne j} J_{ij}\,\svec_i\cdot\svec_j
        -\sum_i K_i\bigl(\svec_i\cdot\unitvect{e}_i\bigr)^2
        \notag\\
       & -\frac{1}{2}\sum_{i\ne j}
           \vect{D}_{ij}\cdot(\svec_i\times\svec_j)
        -\sum_i \vect{\mu}_i\cdot\Bext.
    \label{eq:atomistic-hamiltonian}
\end{align}

\paragraph{Definitions.}
$J_{ij}$ is the exchange coupling, $K_i$ is the uniaxial anisotropy energy,
$\unitvect{e}_i$ is the easy-axis direction, $\vect{D}_{ij}$ is the Dzyaloshinskii--
Moriya vector, and $\vect{\mu}_i$ is the magnetic moment at site $i$.

\paragraph{Assumptions.}
Each $\svec_i$ is a classical unit vector.  The factors of $1/2$ assume that the
pair sums include both $(i,j)$ and $(j,i)$.

\paragraph{Units.}
All terms in $\mathcal{H}$ have units of energy.  Here the external flux density
$\Bext$ is measured in tesla, so no additional factor of $\mu_0$ appears in the
Zeeman term.
