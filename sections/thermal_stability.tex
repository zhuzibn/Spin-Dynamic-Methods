\subsection{Thermal Field in a Macrospin Model}

\paragraph{Correlation.}
For the $\vect{H}$-field convention used in Eq.~\eqref{eq:llg-gilbert}, one common
white-noise convention is
\begin{equation}
    \avg{H_{\mathrm{th},i}(t)H_{\mathrm{th},j}(t')}
    = \frac{2\alpha\kB T}{\gamma_0\Ms V}\,
    \delta_{ij}\,\delta(t-t').
    \label{eq:thermal-field-correlation}
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Implementation notes.}
The discrete-time variance depends on the stochastic calculus convention, the form
of the LLG equation, and the time-step definition.  Derive the sampled-noise amplitude
from the exact solver convention rather than copying Eq.~\eqref{eq:thermal-field-correlation}
without checking it.
